# AdLib - Publish Ads & Earn Crypto

A next-gen advertising platform where advertisers can publish AI-powered ads on the blockchain and publishers can seamlessly integrate them to earn crypto revenue.

# 1. Platform Users

The platform has two main types of users:

- **Advertiser:** Users who create, publish, and personalize ads, and track their performance.

- **Publisher:** Users who integrate ad spaces into their websites and earn revenue from these ads.

# 2. Advertiser Panel Workflow

After logging in, advertisers can:

- **Publish Ads**:

- - Ads are published through a blockchain-based infrastructure.
- - Blockchain ensures transparency and verifiability of ad delivery.

- **AI-Powered Personalization**:

- - Ads can be personalized for target audiences using AI algorithms.
- - AI settings can be managed with a single click.

# 3. Quick Integration:

- Using npm packages or other integration tools, ads can be added to their websites or platforms within seconds.

# 4. Performance Tracking:

- Advertisers can view ad performance statistics directly from the homepage.
